The best hotels in Paris

The best hotels in Paris, chosen by Condé Nast Traveller editors—from boutique boltholes to palace-status grande dames

By Sarah James

15 October 2025

Trust the Parisians to lead the charge when it comes to the most glamorous and stylish hotels in the world. Amongst its majestic boulevards and hidden within its romantic arrondissements are an incredible collection of hotels that are destinations in their own right.

Names like The Ritz, Hôtel Plaza Athénée and Hôtel de Crillon are gilded landmarks, famed for attracting royals and movie stars. Then there are romantic boutique boltholes in townhouses, decorated with a fantastical jeu de vivre that one can only find in Paris. Plan wisely—and take our editors' advice—and a perfect stay is well within reach.

La Réserve Paris Hotel and Spa

42 Av. Gabriel, 75008 Paris, France

Gold List 2026 Readers' Choice

With just 40 rooms, La Réserve is by far the smallest of the city’s palace-designated hotels, and retains a discreet residential quality—silk, velvet, Versailles-style parquet, and views from Notre-Dame to the Eiffel Tower.

Le Bristol Paris

112 Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, 75008 Paris, France

Hot List

Grand, storied and luxurious—from the marble lobby’s vast flower arrangements to the rooftop pool and three Michelin-starred Epicure. Le Bristol celebrates a century of Parisian allure in 2025.

Le Grand Mazarin

17 Rue de la Verrerie, 75004 Paris, France

Martin Brudnizki’s Marais fantasy: a glass atrium winter garden, a rare indoor pool, and Boubalé’s Ashkenazi-tinged menu—boutique Paris at its most seductive.
